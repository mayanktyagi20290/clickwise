Search Console tells you what happened. It doesn't tell you what to do next.

The Performance report is thousands of rows. Finding the ten that matter means exports, pivot tables and regex filters, every month.

Clickwise reads the same data and does that work for you: it separates branded searches from real SEO traffic, compares every search with what its position usually earns on your own site, and ranks the gaps by clicks per month.

What you get

A to-do list ranked by clicks, with honest ranges

Every recommendation comes with a low–high estimate of clicks per month. Estimates use your own site's click rates where there's enough data, count each search once, and flag searches where an answer box is probably taking the click — so you don't chase numbers that won't come.

      Questions

      What access does Clickwise need?

      Read-only access to Search Console (the webmasters.readonly scope). Clickwise can't change anything in your account, and you can revoke access any time from your Google account settings.

      Where is my data stored?

      Nowhere but your browser. Data is fetched from Google straight into the page and analysed there; reloading clears it. Only your settings — brand names, ignored searches, saved workflows — are kept in your browser, and Settings › Forget this site deletes them.

      How are the click estimates worked out?

      For each search, Clickwise compares its click-through rate with what your own site usually earns at that position (an industry benchmark where you don't have enough data), and shows the gap as a low–high range of clicks per month. Each search is counted once, outliers are capped, and searches that look answered on the results page are flagged. Use the numbers to prioritise, not as a forecast.

      Can I use it without signing in with Google?

      Yes. Export the Performance report from Search Console as Excel and upload it. To see pages losing clicks, export with Date › Compare, or upload two exports together. A few details need sign-in, such as which search lands on which page.

      Can I analyse just one section of my site?

      Yes. Type a folder like /blog/ or paste a page URL when you pick your property, and switch to another folder from the top bar at any time. For uploads, set the Page filter in Search Console before exporting.

      Does it work for any website?

      Any site with a Search Console property: blogs, ecommerce, SaaS, marketplaces, B2B. Sections and page types are read from your own URL structure, and brand names from your domain.

      How is this different from Ahrefs or Semrush?

      Those tools estimate your rankings from their own crawls. Clickwise uses Google's first-party data for your site — real impressions, clicks and positions — and focuses on what to fix next rather than keyword research.
