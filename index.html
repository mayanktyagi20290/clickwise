Search Console tells you what happened. It doesn't tell you what to do next.

The Performance report gives you thousands of rows of queries and pages. Finding the ten that matter means exports, pivot tables and a long afternoon, every month.

Clickwise does that work in seconds. It compares every query's click-through rate with what its position should earn, separates branded searches from real SEO traffic, and turns the gap into a number you can prioritise: clicks per month.

Questions

What access does Clickwise need?

Read-only access to Search Console (the webmasters.readonly scope). Clickwise can't change anything in your account, and you can revoke access any time from your Google account settings.

Where is my data stored?

In the free version, nowhere but your browser. Data is fetched from Google directly into the page and analysed there. Your brand terms are saved in your browser so you don't retype them.

How are the “clicks to gain” estimates calculated?

Each query's CTR is compared with a benchmark curve for its position, roughly 28% at #1, 11% at #3 and 2.4% at #10. The gap times impressions is the opportunity. Branded searches are excluded. Use the numbers to prioritise, not as a forecast.

Can I use it without signing in with Google?

Yes. Export the Performance report from Search Console as Excel and upload it. A few reports are richer with sign-in: exact competing-page detection and content decay need data the export doesn't include.

Does it work for any website?

Any site with a verified Search Console property: blogs, ecommerce, SaaS, marketplaces. Site sections are detected from your own URL structure, and brand terms come from your domain.

How is this different from Ahrefs or Semrush?

Those tools estimate your rankings from their own crawls. Clickwise uses Google's first-party data for your site — real impressions, clicks and positions — and focuses on what to fix next rather than on keyword research.
