Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What Clickwise accesses

If you sign in with Google, Clickwise requests read-only access to Google Search Console (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly). It reads the list of properties you can access and search performance data (queries, pages, dates, devices, clicks, impressions, CTR and position) for the property and dates you choose.

How the data is used

Data is requested directly from Google by your browser and analysed in your browser to produce the reports you see. It is not sent to or stored on Clickwise servers. The access token is held in memory and discarded when you close the page.

Brand terms and excluded URL patterns you enter are saved in your browser's local storage so you don't need to retype them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Clickwise's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it.

Revoking access

You can remove Clickwise's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Uploaded files

Files you upload are read in your browser and never transmitted.
